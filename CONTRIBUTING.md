# Contributing

This is a playbook, not a codebase. The most valuable contribution is a **new pitfall with its fix**, or a correction to a cost or timeline that has drifted.

**Before you open a pull request:**

- Keep the structure: one page per topic under `docs/`, numbered. Add to the [consolidated pitfall list](docs/10-pitfalls.md) when you add a trap to a topic page.
- Say what breaks and what the fix is. A symptom without a cause is a half-entry.
- Prices and timelines change. Date them (`as of YYYY-MM`) when you add or update one.
- Never commit credentials, account identifiers, or anything from your own `.env`. Use the `templates/*.example` files for shape only.
- Replace product-specific values with placeholders in angle brackets, e.g. `<brand-name>`, `<SERVER_IP>`.

Open an issue if you are unsure whether something belongs here.
